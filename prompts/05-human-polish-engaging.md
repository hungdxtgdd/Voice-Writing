# ♟️ Phase E: Engaging (Move 78 & The Human Polish)

## 🎯 Purpose
Overcomes **Boring** writing. **There is NO prompt that can replace human taste.** Trimming fillers and injecting rhythmic tension belongs entirely to human judgment.

---

## 👑 The "Move 78" Philosophy
In Game 4 of AlphaGo vs Lee Sedol (2016), Lee played **Move 78**—a move with 0.007% probability that broke the machine's statistical model. In writing, Move 78 is your unique voice, vulnerability, humor, and intuition.

---

## ✅ Human Polish Checklist

- [ ] **The Opening Hook**: Does the first sentence create immediate stakes or curiosity?
- [ ] **Rhythmic Variation**: Alternate short, punchy sentences with longer explanatory ones.
  - *Example*: "AI writes fast. But fast garbage is still garbage."
- [ ] **Cut the Fillers**: Remove phrases like *"In today's fast-paced digital world"*, *"It is important to remember"*, *"Delve into"*, *"Tapestry"*.
- [ ] **The "One More Thing"**: Include one unexpected twist, personal reflection, or counterintuitive takeaway before the conclusion.
