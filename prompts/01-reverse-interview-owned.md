# 🎙️ Phase O: Owned (Dấu ấn sở hữu & Khai thác chất liệu)

## 🎯 Purpose
Overcomes **AI Homogenization** (Cornell 2025) and generic prose. Prevents the AI from defaulting to internet cliches by forcing it to interview the author for primary raw material.

---

## 📋 Prompt Template

```markdown
I want to write about [INSERT TOPIC], but DO NOT WRITE ANYTHING YET.

Act as a veteran investigative journalist and biographer:
1. Interview me one question at a time (ask only ONE question per turn).
2. Dig into my firsthand experiences: specific failures, non-obvious observations, expensive mistakes, or unique case studies.
3. If my answer is vague or overly theoretical, push back and ask for specific details (dates, names, metrics, dialogue).
4. Continue until you have collected at least 3-5 distinct, authentic details from my personal or professional experience.
```

---

## 💡 Best Practices
- **Never skip this step**: AI cannot invent your lived experience.
- **Audio-first option**: You can record your raw thoughts on voice memo and transcribe them into bullet points before feeding them in.
