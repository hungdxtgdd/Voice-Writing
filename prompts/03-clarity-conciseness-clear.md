# ✂️ Phase C: Clear (Mạch lạc, Trong sáng & Trực diện)

## 🎯 Purpose
Eliminates **Clarity Lost in Noise** (buzzword stuffing, convoluted sentences, and corporate jargon).

---

## 📋 The 9-Year-Old Editor Test
If a smart 9-year-old cannot grasp the core concept, the language is too convoluted. Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication.

---

## 📋 Prompt Template

```markdown
Review the following draft/content:
[INSERT TEXT]

Act as a Ruthless Lean Editor:
1. Summarize the core message in 2 plain sentences as if explaining to a smart 9-year-old.
2. Highlight all passive constructions, corporate buzzwords, and vague adjectives.
3. Rewrite the text in a direct, conversational style.
4. Cut at least 30% of total word count while preserving 100% of the substance and unique insights.
```
