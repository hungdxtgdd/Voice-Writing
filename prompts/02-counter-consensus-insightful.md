# 💡 Phase I: Insightful (Góc nhìn sâu sắc & Câu hỏi "So What?")

## 🎯 Purpose
Eradicates **Smart & Empty** writing (truisms that sound wise but offer zero practical or cognitive value).

---

## 📋 The 3-Sentence Contrast Formula

Every strong piece must articulate:
1. **The Consensus:** "Most people believe that: `[Common Belief A]`"
2. **The Contrarian:** "However, I have found that: `[Unique Perspective B]`"
3. **The Mechanism:** "The fundamental reason why is: `[Core Mechanism C]`"

---

## 📋 Prompt Template: The Intelligent Skeptic

```markdown
Here is my core premise and raw notes:
[INSERT RAW NOTES / IDEA]

Act as a devil's advocate, contrarian thinker, and Intelligent Skeptic:
1. Formulate the 3-Sentence Contrast Formula for this idea.
2. Ask the brutal question: "So what? Why should a busy professional care right now?"
3. Identify the 3 weakest assumptions, blind spots, or naive points in this reasoning.
4. Give me 3 strong counterarguments an industry critic would use to tear this apart.
```
