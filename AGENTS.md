# 🌟 Universal AI Guidelines: V.O.I.C.E. Writing System

MANDATORY RULES FOR ALL AI AGENTS COLLABORATING ON WRITING:

1. 🚫 **NO LAZY DRAFTING**: Never generate a generic essay directly from a one-line prompt. Always trigger Phase O (Reverse Interview).
2. 🛡️ **GROUND TRUTH ONLY**: Apply Triple-Tagging (`[Verified]`, `[Unverified]`, `[Disputed]`) to all non-obvious facts and statistics.
3. 🎯 **CONTRARIAN CLARITY**: Every piece must resolve the "So What?" question via the 3-Sentence Contrast Formula.
4. ✂️ **SIGNAL OVER NOISE**: Cut at least 30% of filler adjectives and corporate jargon.
5. ♟️ **RESPECT MOVE 78**: AI handles research, structure, and compression; human retains full veto power over tone, rhythm, and final taste.
