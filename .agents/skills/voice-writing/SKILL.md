# 🎙️ V.O.I.C.E. Writing Framework Skill

Use this skill whenever drafting, revising, or reviewing any form of writing (articles, essays, newsletters, LinkedIn posts, technical documentation) to avoid AI-generated generic blandness.

## 🔄 The 5-Phase State Machine

When a writing task is initiated, execute these phases in exact sequential order:

```
[Phase O: Owned] ──> [Phase I: Insightful] ──> [Phase C: Clear] ──> [Phase V: Verified] ──> [Phase E: Engaging]
 (Reverse Interview)   (Contrast & Skeptic)     (Draft & 9-Year-Old)    (Triple-Tagging)      (Human Move 78)
```

---

### Phase 1: O – OWNED (Reverse Interviewer Mode)
**Objective**: Prevent generic tone by extracting lived experiences before drafting.
- **Rule**: NEVER draft immediately upon receiving a broad topic.
- **Action**: Act as an investigative journalist. Ask **ONE question at a time**.
- **Target**: Extract at least 3-5 concrete details (past mistakes, specific numbers, field observations, personal anecdotes).
- **Completion Criteria**: Proceed to Phase 2 only when sufficient unique human raw material is collected.

---

### Phase 2: I – INSIGHTFUL (Intelligent Skeptic Mode)
**Objective**: Eradicate truisms and surface-level clichés.
- **Action 1**: Construct the **3-Sentence Contrast Formula**:
  - *Consensus*: "Most people believe that: [Common Belief A]"
  - *Contrarian*: "However, the reality is: [Unique Perspective B]"
  - *Mechanism*: "The fundamental divergence is: [Core Reason C]"
- **Action 2**: Run the **"So What?" Test**. Challenge the premise with 3 harsh counterarguments an industry veteran would pose.

---

### Phase 3: C – CLEAR (Drafting & Simplification)
**Objective**: Maximize signal-to-noise ratio and readability.
- **Action 1**: Write the initial draft adhering strictly to the material from Phase O & I.
- **Action 2**: Run the **9-Year-Old Test**:
  - Highlight abstract jargon or ambiguous phrasing.
  - Convert passive voice to active voice.
  - Cut at least **30% of unnecessary filler words** without losing core message.

---

### Phase 4: V – VERIFIED (Fact-Checking Editor Mode)
**Objective**: Eliminate AI hallucinations and ungrounded claims.
- **Action**: Extract all factual claims, metrics, dates, and names into a **Triple-Tagging Table**:

| Claim in Text | Tag ([Verified] / [Unverified] / [Disputed]) | Primary Source / Proof |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

- **Rule**: Flag any unverified claims clearly to the user for confirmation or removal.

---

### Phase 5: E – ENGAGING (Human "Move 78" Hand-off)
**Objective**: Infuse human taste, tension, and rhythmic pacing.
- **Action**: Present the refined draft to the human author along with the **Move 78 Polish Checklist**:
  - [ ] Opening hook creates stakes/curiosity.
  - [ ] Pacing alternates between punchy short sentences and explanatory long sentences.
  - [ ] "One More Thing" moment: an unexpected insight or memorable takeaway.
